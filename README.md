# HydroGuard – Industrial Water Intelligence

## Problem Statement

Industries consume large amounts of water during production. A sudden increase in water consumption may be caused by increased production or by an abnormal condition such as a hidden water leak.

Traditional monitoring methods may identify excessive consumption only after significant water and financial losses have occurred.

The challenge is to develop a smart system that can monitor water usage, distinguish production-related consumption from abnormal usage, identify the probable affected zone, estimate water loss and financial impact, and provide clear alerts to operators.

---

## Our Solution

**HydroGuard** is a production-aware industrial water monitoring and leak detection prototype.

The system analyzes multiple signals:

- Water flow
- Water pressure
- Production activity
- Water consumption

Instead of looking at water consumption alone, HydroGuard compares multiple signals to identify abnormal patterns.

When an abnormal pattern is detected, the system:

1. Detects the anomaly
2. Identifies the probable affected zone
3. Calculates estimated water loss
4. Estimates financial impact
5. Displays the information on a real-time dashboard
6. Provides simulated response actions
